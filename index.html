Stage 01 · On-demand interview

Tell your story with evidence.

Practice 6 plausible competency questions. Aim for 90–120 seconds spoken per answer. Use STAR-L for past examples and a risk-action-evidence structure for scenarios.

~15–20 min
Strong answer structure: Answer directly → give a specific example or decision sequence → explain your personal actions → state the result → connect the learning to this role. The recruiter is looking for judgment and evidence, not memorised slogans.

Your answer should connect the WBG mission to the day-to-day work: risk screening, evidence review, monitoring, writing and teamwork.

Stage 03 · Technical and situational judgment

Choose the best next action.

One best answer per question. Read the case facts carefully. The score and explanations stay hidden until you reach the final review.

8 questions · 20 min
Decision principle: Use a risk-based approach. Prioritise credible threats to people and the environment, verify material claims, match action to the severity and likelihood of impact, and record clear responsibilities and follow-up.
Stage 04 · Virtual job tryout

Do the work, not just describe it.

Complete the practical tasks as if you were supporting the CCMES E&S team. Strong responses are concise, evidence-based and actionable.

~25 min
Task A · E&S screening noteSuggested: 5 min

Prepare a short initial screening note for the logistics corridor project.

Identify key potential impacts, affected groups, material unknowns, likely relevant Performance Standards and the priority documents or studies to request. Avoid making a final risk category without adequate information.

Focus: risk identification and evidence gaps0 words
Task B · ESAP action qualitySuggested: 5 min

Rewrite this weak action into a trackable ESAP action: “The client should improve community safety soon.”

Include the deliverable, timing or milestone, accountable party, measurable acceptance criteria and evidence of completion. Keep the action proportionate to the risk.

Focus: specific, measurable, time-bound and verifiable0 words
Task C · Incident briefingSuggested: 5 min

A contractor reports a chemical spill near a watercourse. Volume and downstream effects are unknown. Draft an initial note to the E&S lead.

Separate confirmed facts from unknowns. Cover immediate protection, notification, evidence preservation, investigation, corrective action and follow-up.

Focus: people and environment first0 words
Task D · Annual Monitoring Report reviewSuggested: 5 min

The AMR marks all ESAP actions “on track,” but three are overdue, two closed actions have no evidence, and a contractor incident appears only in an appendix. What would your review note say?

Focus: verification, trend and follow-through0 words
Task E · Financial intermediarySuggested: 5 min
